ABOUT

About Me

I'm a videographer and video editor based in Berkshire, UK, with over 8 years of experience creating and editing video content. Over the years, I've worked with sport teams, professional athletes, academies, E-sport teams and Premier League coaches, as well as creating travel and lifestyle content.

I specialise in creating engaging visual content, from cinematic videos and sports coverage to social media content and promotional campaigns. I have extensive experience using Adobe After Effects and Premiere Pro.

Alongside my editing skills, I use professional Sony camera equipment to capture high-quality footage, with a focus on composition, storytelling and visual detail.

I'm passionate about creating content that captures attention, tells a story and helps individuals, teams and organisations build their brand. I'm always looking to take on new projects, work with new people and bring creative ideas to life.
